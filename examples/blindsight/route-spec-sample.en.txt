N 20 Inattentional blindness is a real psychological effect. The best-known experiment is the "invisible gorilla": people counting passes in a basketball video, and about half of them miss a person in a gorilla suit walking through the middle of the scene. The author says one key feature of the scramblers comes from research like this.
N 22 This passage states one of the book's core ideas: what we see is not the world but a model inside the brain, which the senses keep updating. When the model breaks, a person lives in a world detached from reality and does not notice.
